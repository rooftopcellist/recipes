Hot Honey Chicken

Serves: 3–4

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 3½ hours on High

Ingredients

  • ½ cup honey
  • ½ lime, juiced
  • 2 tablespoons Sriracha
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1½ teaspoons salt
  • ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs (6 to 8 thighs)
  • Cooked white rice, for serving

Directions

  1. Make the sauce: Set a slow cooker to High. In a medium bowl, whisk the honey, lime juice, Sriracha, oil, garlic, salt, and pepper until fully combined.
  2. Add the chicken: Place the chicken thighs in the slow cooker and pour the sauce over the chicken, distributing it evenly.
  3. Cook: Cook on High until the chicken is tender and cooked through (the internal temperature should reach 180°F), about 3½ hours. Flip the chicken halfway through cooking so each side gets coated in the sauce.
  4. Serve: Serve over rice with toppings of your choice.

Storage and Reheating

Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days or in the freezer for up to 2 months. Reheat in the microwave in 30-second increments until warmed through.

Enjoy!
